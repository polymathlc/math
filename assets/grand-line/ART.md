# Grand Line character artwork

Generated with the built-in image_gen tool. Each unchanged RGBA PNG contains a painted character scene on the left and its matching full-body battle avatar on actual transparency on the right. Manifest rectangles follow the measured alpha boundary. The game renders frames, foil, rarity stars and labels separately.

Expansion II adds 50 characters, including seven-star Charlotte Linlin (Big Mom), Monkey D. Garp and Sabo under separate edition IDs. Existing Wapol, Don Krieg and Gin art remains current; retired legacy artwork is retained only in the creation workspace. Card scenes deliberately vary camera angle and composition.

Generation prompts are retained beside every original PNG. Importing copies the original bytes. Delivery encoding is lossless and verifies every visible RGB value and alpha value; no artwork or background is painted, removed or retouched by code.
