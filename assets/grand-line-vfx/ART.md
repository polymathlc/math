# Crew Defense animation artwork

Generated with the built-in image generation tool. Twelve dedicated atlases cover all three skills of every current six- and seven-star character. The shared generic atlas supplies strikes, impacts and support pulses for the other 88 characters. Each atlas contains three rows of four sequential frames. Thirteen active atlases provide 156 frames; the archived Law sheet remains in delivery history and is never selected or loaded by gameplay.

The expansion adds Big Mom, Garp, Sabo, Rayleigh and Oden. Garp uses Haki-powered Iron Fist, Blue Hole and Galaxy Impact; his effects do not depict a gravity Devil Fruit. All five new originals and their prompts are retained in the creation workspace. Runtime images use lossless WebP encoding; every visible RGB pixel and alpha value was verified against its generated PNG. No background removal or repainting was performed outside the image generator.
