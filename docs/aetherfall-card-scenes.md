# Aetherfall card-scene art direction

Every card shows a character in a real place. The environment remains deliberately quiet: one landmark, one ground plane, and at most one distant layer. It gives the illustration narrative context without competing with the silhouette or making the set look procedurally overdesigned.

The roster atlas remains the source of truth, but every grid cell is isolated to its largest central silhouette before export. This prevents a neighboring head, weapon, glow, or wing from spilling into another card or battle avatar. Students keep the same collectible IDs, rarities, and ownership records.

## Meridian Exodus locations

- Crown Observatory — an ivory-and-brass terrace looking across a Dyson ring.
- Living Laboratory — a regenerative-biotech chamber where architecture and cultivated life meet.
- Wormgate Concourse — the calm transit hall used during the Meridian Exodus.
- Ashen Colony — a restrained reminder that Aethari progress sometimes damaged living worlds.
- Astral Archive — a star-map alcove holding centuries of navigational memory.
- Devourer Rift — a sparse viewing ledge at the edge of the cosmic Hunger's dimensional wound.

## Mana World locations

- Viridian Rootway — a forest road beneath the roots of Elarion's oldest living structures.
- Tideglass Shrine — a quiet coastal platform used by waterwardens and pilgrims.
- Emberpeak Forge — the mountain smithy that equips the fire clans and highholds.
- Frostglass Pass — a severe ice road between the northern kingdoms.
- Sunspire Highhold — a warm limestone terrace overlooking the central hills.
- Umbral Fen Ruins — a drowned causeway where shadow mana gathers in old stone.

## Image-generation prompt pattern

The twelve plates were generated as square, character-free production assets with the same constraints: a polished Western game-illustration finish; exactly one simple location; two or three broad shapes; a quiet central subject area; no people, creatures, text, frames, UI, particles, battle effects, ornate clutter, flat colour field, or pure gradient. The apex Seraphine source used a background-only edit with the character, pose, armor, face, hair, crown, and effects explicitly locked.
