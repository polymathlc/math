    ALLIANCES & LEGENDS
    Pack odds & automatic merging

    Each pack contains one character card. Choose 1, 5, 10, or 50 packs in a purchase; every pack uses the price and rarity odds of the selected tier. Prices and rarity guarantees follow your platform’s existing Bronze, Silver, and Gold TCG packs. Rarity is drawn first; characters within that rarity are equally likely. Seven-star cards include Kaido the Beast, Whitebeard, Admiral Akainu, Big Mom, Garp, and Sabo. Star ratings are fixed card rarities. Duplicates increase merge rank at 2, 4, 8, 16… total copies, up to rank 10. Every merge rank adds 12% to base life, attack, and defense.
