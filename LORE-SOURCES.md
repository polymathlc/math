# Grand Line TCG — lore anchors

Reviewed 15 September 2026. Primary sources: the official ONE PIECE character directory and Bandai Namco official character features. Search-index excerpts were used where the official site rejected direct fetches.

Character identities, fruit families, weapons and fighting themes follow these sources. The 50-card roster, ratings, statistical passives, costs, durations, status translations and some move labels are original fan-game adaptations. Seven-star rarity is an expansion rule, not an official power ranking. All battle revivals mean recovery from a knockout, not reversal of canonical deaths. Campaign enemy lineups are dream-match encounters rather than a retelling of canonical alliances. Admiral Akainu is the requested Marineford-era card title; Sakazuki is currently identified as fleet admiral by his official profile.

Additional primary anchors for the replacement abilities: the official synopsis for [episode 171](https://one-piece.com/anime/171/index.html) describes Wyper’s blue-white Burn Bazooka flame, and [episode 169](https://one-piece.com/anime/169/index.html) identifies his body-straining Reject Dial strike. The numerical recoil penalty, barriers, damage-over-time effects, Spirit drain, evasion and other combat statistics translate these themes into this game’s shared rules.

Mechanics and economy: packs are purchased only through the host platform reward-point shop at its existing rates. Correct answers in three-question rounds grant recovery and attack, critical-chance and defense bonuses for the next round; no question-to-pack or free-pack economy is implemented.
