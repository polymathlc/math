# Grand Line TCG — lore anchors · 3.4.0

Reviewed 15 September 2026. Primary sources: the official ONE PIECE character directory and Bandai Namco official character features. Search-index excerpts were used where the official site rejected direct fetches.

Character identities, fruit families, weapons and fighting themes use these primary sources as anchors. The 100-card roster, ratings, statistical passives, costs, durations, status translations and some move labels are original fan-game adaptations. Seven-star rarity is an expansion rule, not an official power ranking. All battle revivals mean recovery from a knockout, not reversal of canonical deaths. Campaign enemy lineups are dream-match encounters rather than a retelling of canonical alliances. Admiral Akainu is the requested Marineford-era card title. Current and historical affiliations can coexist as collection tags; they do not assert that a character served every group at the same time.

### Named techniques and historical affiliations

Big Mom's dedicated abilities combine Napoleon, flame and lightning: **Napoleon: Cognac**, **Ikoku Sovereignty** and **Misery: Homie Fusion**. Their primary anchors are the official synopses for [Cognac](https://one-piece.com/anime/o4907/index.html), [Ikoku](https://one-piece.com/anime/o4765/index.html) and [Misery](https://one-piece.com/anime/62293/index.html). Burn, armor penetration and short stun effects are game adaptations of those attacks.

Garp's **Iron Fist**, **Blue Hole** and **Galaxy Impact** emphasize physical strikes and Haki shockwaves. [Bandai's official Garp feature](https://optc-ww.channel.or.jp/news/562/) names Galaxy Impact, while the [official episode synopsis](https://one-piece.com/anime/68212/index.html) names Blue Hole. His area shockwave uses the game's impact effect family; it does not assign him gravity or a Devil Fruit. Damage multipliers and armor penetration are this game's balance choices, not copied stats from Bandai's game.

Sabo's **Dragon Claw Fist**, **Fire Fist** and **Flame Dragon King** combine his martial art with flame abilities, anchored by the [official Sabo battle synopsis](https://one-piece.com/anime/o2851/index.html). The label and attack footprint of Flame Dragon King adapt that combination. Burn immunity and the nearby attack-speed aura are fan-game mechanics.

The official [Rocks profile](https://one-piece.com/character/Rocks_D_Xebec/index.html) identifies Whitebeard, Big Mom and Kaido as former crew members. Their Rocks tags coexist with their own later crews. The official [Oden feature](https://one-piece.com/greg/o20170222_0695/index.html) anchors his Whitebeard and Roger crew history; the game also tags his Kozuki affiliation. These historical connections support formation choices without rewriting the story's timeline.

Additional expansion references include [Morley's official profile](https://one-piece.com/character/Morley/index.html) for the Push-Push Fruit, the [Garp and Grus episode synopsis](https://one-piece.com/anime/68048/index.html) for fist shockwaves and clay golems, and the [Clay Web synopsis](https://one-piece.com/anime/69263/index.html) for Grus protecting a Marine ship. Clay shields, terrain-shaped area hits and control durations translate these themes into the shared defense rules.

## Formation rules and illustrated effects

The active formation holds **one to seven distinct owned characters**. Two, three or five members sharing an allegiance give its matching members **+6%, +10% or +16% life, attack, defense and speed**. Multiple qualifying allegiances add per character, capped at **30%**. This numerical synergy is an original game rule, not an official power comparison. It does not require a captain. Captain badges reflect lore roles; several captains may coexist. Some captains and commanders have separate nearby auras, so a Marine or Revolutionary leader is not automatically labeled a pirate captain.

Leader auras cover living allies within **240 battlefield units (six grid cells as a radius)**, including their source and independent of allegiance. Only the strongest aura of each stat applies, capped at **12% attack and 12% attack speed**. Leaving range or losing the leader removes the effect; maze towers and enemies receive none. Aura labels and percentages are original mechanics. The UI distinguishes active allegiance bonuses, received auras and out-of-range leaders. Exact leader values are listed in [GRAND-LINE.md](GRAND-LINE.md#allegiances-captains-and-nearby-auras).

Older formations keep their first seven valid distinct owned members in order after legacy conversions. Cards excluded from the smaller active formation remain owned with every duplicate copy intact. Legacy copies, team slots and single- or multi-pack receipts resolve to the permanent replacement IDs without another roll or charge. Existing five-member crews remain valid.

The active VFX roster contains **12 premium characters with 36 dedicated ability rows** and 88 characters using shared animations. Thirteen active sheets contain **156 frames**. All 100 characters retain distinct skill data and attack profiles even where lower-rarity effects are shared. Law's archived artwork and effects do not make his reserved future card obtainable. Generated art and effects are fan-game illustrations; source prompts and hashes are recorded in the corresponding asset provenance files.

Mechanics and economy: packs are purchased only through the host platform reward-point shop at its existing rates, in batches of 1, 5, 10 or 50 packs with one card per pack. Three-question rounds award training points; correct answers also improve attack, critical chance and defense for the next wave. No question-to-pack or free-pack economy is implemented. Administrator-only unlimited gold and unlock-all remain separate authorized account controls.
